# NameplateQuestInfo

NameplateQuestInfo helps you spot quest mobs in WoW Forever Beta. It automatically adds a yellow quest marker to the left of their nameplates and shows your objective progress, such as **3/10**, on the right for kill and quest-item objectives.

## Getting started

Install NameplateQuestInfo in your `Interface/AddOns` folder and enable it in the AddOns menu. Quest markers and progress appear automatically when the addon loads. No commands or configuration are needed.

Nameplates must be visible for NameplateQuestInfo to display markers. Enable friendly nameplates when looking for friendly quest mobs.

Disable the addon in the AddOns menu to turn off its display. NameplateQuestInfo works on its own and does not require any other addons.

The addon uses a golden quest exclamation mark as its icon in the AddOns menu.
